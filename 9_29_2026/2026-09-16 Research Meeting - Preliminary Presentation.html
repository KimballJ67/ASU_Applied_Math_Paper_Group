3 Differences between Treatment Branches

I believe that a model of combination CAR T-cell and OV therapies would be insufficient for mRNA regime because:

  1. Timing
  2. Processes
  3. Adaptive Memory

Difference 1: Timing

Donor T-Cells are activated before injection. They begin as effector cells. Vaccine-derived effector cells come following a process of priming and activation.

Difference 2: Processes

  • Activation, recruitment, and proliferation
  • Transport in the lymphatic system
  • Availability of precursor
  • Contraction (?)

Difference 3: Adaptive Memory

  • Appears in the data, in the results of a 14 day boost.
  • But may not significantly matter in this timeframe
  • Not sure.

Bottom line - I am imagining a need for a different mathematical representation
